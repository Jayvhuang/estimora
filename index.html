How Estimora works

Every estimate follows the same three steps — the same ones an estimator uses on site.

1. Geometry

Your dimensions become an area or a volume. Openings such as doors and windows are subtracted where they matter.

2. Coverage

That quantity is divided by the coverage rate of the material — square feet per gallon, cubic feet per bag, tiles per box.

3. Waste & rounding

A waste factor covers cuts, breakage and offcuts. The result is rounded up to whole bags, boxes, sheets or bundles, because that is how materials are sold.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are these estimates?估算结果有多准？

The geometry is exact — areas and volumes come straight from the dimensions you type. The uncertainty is in the coverage rates: paint spread rate, bag yield, tiles per box and shingle coverage all vary by product. Estimora uses industry-typical defaults and lets you override the ones that matter, so check the packaging of what you actually buy.几何部分是精确的——面积和体积直接由你输入的尺寸算出。不确定性来自覆盖率：涂料涂布率、每袋产出量、每箱砖数、每捆瓦覆盖面积都会因产品而异。Estimora 使用行业常见默认值，并允许你修改关键参数，请以实际购买产品的包装标注为准。

Can I type feet and inches?可以直接输入英尺和英寸吗？

Yes. Every length field accepts 12' 6", 12 ft 6 in, 150", 1 1/2", 3.8 m, 380 cm, or a plain number in the active unit system. Switching between imperial and metric converts what you already typed instead of wiping it.可以。所有长度输入框都能识别 12' 6"、12 ft 6 in、150"、1 1/2"、3.8 m、380 cm，或按当前单位制输入的纯数字。切换英制／公制时会自动换算已输入的数值，而不是清空重填。

Why round up to whole bags and boxes?为什么要向上取整到整袋、整箱？

Because that is how materials are sold. 7.2 bags means you buy 8. Estimora shows the raw quantity and the purchasable quantity side by side, so the rounding is visible rather than hidden.因为材料就是按整包卖的。算出 7.2 袋就必须买 8 袋。Estimora 会同时显示原始用量和实际采购量，让取整过程一目了然。

What waste factor should I use?损耗率应该设多少？

10% is a common default for straightforward rectangular work. Go higher for diagonal tile layouts, complex roofs, patterned flooring or a first DIY attempt. Go lower only if you are confident about your cut list.常规矩形施工通常取 10%。斜铺瓷砖、复杂屋面、需要对花的地板，或第一次自己动手时应调高。只有在切割方案很有把握时才调低。

Is anything uploaded to a server?数据会上传到服务器吗？

No. Everything is computed in JavaScript in your browser. Your dimensions never leave the device and are not stored. Only your language, theme and unit-system choices are kept in local storage.不会。所有计算都在你浏览器里用 JavaScript 完成。尺寸数据不会离开你的设备，也不会被保存。只有语言、主题和单位制偏好会存在本地存储里。

Can I rely on the stair tool for code compliance?楼梯工具能作为合规依据吗？

Use it for planning. It flags results against commonly used residential limits (max riser 7¾ in, min tread 10 in), but codes differ by jurisdiction and edition. Confirm with your local building department before you cut a stringer.只能用于方案规划。工具会按常见住宅规范限值（最大踢面 7¾ 英寸、最小踏面 10 英寸）给出提示，但各地规范与版本不同。开料切斜梁之前，请以当地主管部门执行的规范为准。

Does it work in Chinese and in metric?支持中文和公制吗？

Yes — the entire interface, every result label and every formula note switches between English and 简体中文, and each tool runs in metres and centimetres as readily as in feet and inches.支持。整站界面、所有结果标签和公式说明都可在英文与简体中文之间切换，每个工具也都能用米／厘米运行，和英尺／英寸完全等价。
