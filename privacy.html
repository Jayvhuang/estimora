Privacy Policy隐私政策

Last updated: 31 July 2026最后更新：2026 年 7 月 31 日

This policy explains what Estimora ("we", "the site") does and does not collect when you use estimora.com. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past. 本政策说明你在使用 estimora.com 时，Estimora（以下称"我们"、"本站"）会收集和不会收集哪些信息。我们希望你真的能读完，而不是随手划过。

1. The calculations never leave your device1. 计算数据不会离开你的设备

Every estimator on Estimora runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The dimensions, quantities, coverage rates and waste factors you type are processed locally and are never transmitted to us or to any third party. We operate no account system, no database of user projects, and no upload endpoint. Estimora 上的所有计算器完全在你的浏览器中通过 JavaScript 运行。你输入的尺寸、数量、覆盖率、损耗率均在本地处理，不会被发送给我们或任何第三方。本站没有账号体系、没有存放用户项目的数据库，也没有任何上传接口。

2. Local storage on your own device2. 存放在你本机的数据

We store three preferences in your browser's local storage so the site remembers how you like it: your interface language (estimora-lang), your light/dark theme (estimora-theme) and your unit system (estimora-sys). These stay on your device, are readable only by this site, and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser's site data. 我们会在浏览器的 local storage 中保存三项偏好设置，以便下次访问时保持一致：界面语言（estimora-lang）、浅色／深色主题（estimora-theme）以及单位制（estimora-sys）。这些数据只存在你的设备上、只有本站可读，你随时可以通过清除浏览器站点数据将其删除。

Estimora may display advertising supplied by Google, including through Google AdSense. When advertising is enabled, the following applies: Estimora 可能展示由 Google 提供的广告，包括通过 Google AdSense 投放。在广告功能启用的情况下，以下条款适用：

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. 包括 Google 在内的第三方供应商会使用 Cookie，根据你此前对本站或其他网站的访问记录来投放广告。
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Google 使用广告 Cookie，使其及其合作伙伴能够基于你对本站和／或互联网上其他站点的访问，向你展示广告。
  • These vendors may also use web beacons (also called pixel tags or clear GIFs) — tiny invisible images embedded in a page or an ad — to count ad impressions, measure whether an ad led to an action, and gather statistics about ad performance. 这些供应商还可能使用 网络信标（web beacon，也称像素标签或透明 GIF）——嵌入在页面或广告中的微小不可见图片——用于统计广告展示次数、衡量广告是否带来了后续行为，并收集广告效果数据。
  • In the course of serving ads, third-party vendors automatically receive your IP address, together with your browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, and the date and time of the request. An IP address may be used to infer approximate geographic location (typically at city level) so that regionally relevant ads can be shown, and to detect fraudulent or invalid traffic. 在投放广告的过程中，第三方供应商会自动获得你的 IP 地址，以及浏览器类型、设备类型、操作系统、来源页面和请求时间。IP 地址可能被用于推断大致地理位置（通常精确到城市级别），以便投放与地区相关的广告，并用于识别欺诈或无效流量。
  • We do not control these third-party technologies and we do not receive the personal data they collect. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies. 我们无法控制上述第三方技术，也不会取得它们所收集的个人数据。这些数据的处理受各第三方自身隐私政策约束。

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can review how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and see the list of third-party vendors and their privacy policies at https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/. To opt out of third-party vendor cookies more broadly, visit aboutads.info or networkadvertising.org. 你可以访问 Google 广告设置 关闭个性化广告。关于 Google 如何使用来自其合作网站的信息，可查阅 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites；第三方供应商名单及其隐私政策可查阅 https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/。若要更广泛地关闭第三方供应商 Cookie，可访问 aboutads.info 或 networkadvertising.org。

This privacy page itself loads no advertising script and displays no ads. 本隐私政策页面本身不加载任何广告脚本，也不展示广告。

4. Affiliate links4. 联盟推广链接

Estimora participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. Some outbound product links carry an affiliate tag, and we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you. Clicking such a link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon's own privacy policy and cookies apply. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" and disclosed on the page where they appear. Estimora 参与 Amazon Services LLC Associates Program（亚马逊联盟计划）。部分商品外链带有联盟标识，符合条件的购买可能为我们带来佣金，但不会增加你的支付金额。点击此类链接后你将进入亚马逊网站，届时适用亚马逊自身的隐私政策与 Cookie。所有联盟链接均标注 rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"，并在出现的页面上作出披露。

5. Server logs5. 服务器日志

Estimora is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like virtually all web hosts, the provider records standard request logs that include the requesting IP address, user agent, requested path and timestamp. These logs are used for delivering the site, security and abuse prevention, and are handled by the hosting provider under its own policy. We do not build user profiles from them. Estimora 以静态文件形式由托管服务商提供访问。与几乎所有 Web 主机一样，服务商会记录标准请求日志，其中包含请求方 IP 地址、User-Agent、请求路径和时间戳。这些日志用于站点分发、安全与滥用防护，由托管服务商按其自身政策处理。我们不会用这些日志构建用户画像。

6. Children6. 未成年人

Estimora is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Estimora 是面向一般用户的实用工具，并非针对 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. Your choices7. 你的选择

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the opt-out links in section 3, or use a content blocker. Blocking advertising cookies does not affect any calculator on this site — every tool works with scripts from third parties fully blocked. 你可以在浏览器设置中屏蔽或删除 Cookie，使用第 3 节提供的退出链接，或使用内容拦截插件。屏蔽广告 Cookie 不会影响本站任何计算器——即便完全拦截第三方脚本，所有工具依然可以正常使用。

8. Changes and contact8. 政策变更与联系方式

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@estimora.com. 如本政策发生变更，上方"最后更新"日期会同步更新。有任何疑问，可发送邮件至 privacy@estimora.com。
