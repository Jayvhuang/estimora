Gravel, Sand & Mulch Calculator碎石、沙与覆盖物用量计算器

Loose material is ordered by weight but spread by depth, and the bridge between the two is bulk density. Enter the area and the depth you want, pick or type the density, and this tool gives you the cubic yards to order and the tons that will arrive.散状材料按重量下单，却按厚度铺设，连接两者的是堆积密度。输入面积和目标厚度，选择或填入密度，本工具就会给出下单用的方量和实际到货的吨位。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Volume = area × depth体积 = 面积 × 厚度

Weight = volume × bulk density重量 = 体积 × 堆积密度

Bags = volume ÷ bag volume, rounded up袋数 = 体积 ÷ 每袋体积，向上取整

Bulk densities vary with moisture and grading. Dry crushed stone is roughly 100 lb/ft³ (1,600 kg/m³), damp sand nearer 110 lb/ft³, screened topsoil about 80 lb/ft³ and bark mulch as little as 25 lb/ft³. Two inches is a typical depth for decorative gravel over landscape fabric; a drivable base course is normally 4 in or more and needs compacting in layers, which consumes about 20% more material than the loose volume suggests.堆积密度随含水率和级配变化很大。干碎石约 100 lb/ft³（1600 kg/m³），潮湿的沙接近 110 lb/ft³，过筛种植土约 80 lb/ft³，树皮覆盖物可低至 25 lb/ft³。装饰碎石铺在防草布上通常做 2 英寸厚；可行车的基层一般 4 英寸以上，且需分层压实，压实后的实际用量比松散体积多约 20%。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

How many tons of gravel in a cubic yard?一立方码碎石有多少吨？

About 1.35 tons for dry crushed stone at 100 lb/ft³, since a cubic yard is 27 ft³. Wet material is heavier, so a rain-soaked delivery genuinely weighs more for the same volume.干碎石按 100 lb/ft³ 计，1 立方码（27 ft³）约 1.35 短吨。材料含水后更重，所以淋过雨的同体积货确实会更重。

Bags or bulk delivery?买袋装还是散装？

Bagged material makes sense below about half a cubic yard. Above that, bulk is dramatically cheaper per unit — a cubic yard is roughly 40 half-cubic-foot bags, which is a lot of lifting for no saving.低于约半立方码时买袋装比较合适。超过这个量，散装单价便宜得多——1 立方码相当于约 40 袋 0.5 ft³ 装，搬运量巨大却省不到钱。

Should I add extra for compaction?需要为压实加量吗？

Yes, if the layer will be compacted. Add about 20% for a base course that gets plate-compacted; decorative mulch and loose gravel need no compaction allowance.如果这层材料需要压实，就要加量。用平板夯压实的基层约加 20%；装饰性覆盖物和松铺碎石则无需考虑压实余量。
