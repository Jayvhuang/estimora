Roofing Calculator屋面材料计算器

A roof is always bigger than the house it sits on. The pitch factor is the multiplier between the footprint you can measure from the ground and the sloped area you actually have to cover — a 6:12 roof is 12% larger than its footprint, and a 12:12 roof is 41% larger.屋面永远比房子的投影大。坡度系数就是「从地面量到的投影面积」与「实际要铺的斜面面积」之间的换算系数——6:12 坡度的屋面比投影大 12%，12:12 坡度则大 41%。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Pitch factor = √(rise² + 12²) ÷ 12坡度系数 = √(rise² + 12²) ÷ 12

Roof area = footprint area × pitch factor屋面面积 = 投影面积 × 坡度系数

Squares = roof area ÷ 100 ft²; bundles = squares × 3Square 数 = 屋面面积 ÷ 100 ft²；瓦捆数 = Square × 3

One roofing square is 100 ft² (9.29 m²) and standard three-tab or architectural shingles come three bundles to the square. Add waste for hips and valleys, where every course is cut on an angle — 10% on a simple gable, 15% or more on a cut-up roof. This tool estimates material only; it does not evaluate structural capacity, and adding a second layer of shingles is restricted or prohibited in many jurisdictions.1 个 Square 等于 100 ft²（9.29 m²），常规三片式或叠合瓦通常 3 捆 = 1 Square。斜脊和天沟处每一排瓦都要斜切，需增加损耗——简单双坡屋面按 10%，造型复杂的屋面按 15% 以上。本工具只估算材料，不评估结构承载力；很多地区对「加铺第二层瓦」有限制甚至禁止。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

What is a roofing square?什么是屋面 Square？

100 square feet of finished roof surface — the unit roofers quote in. Shingles are packaged so that three bundles cover one square.指 100 平方英尺的完成屋面，是屋面工程报价的计量单位。沥青瓦通常按 3 捆覆盖 1 个 Square 包装。

How do I find my roof pitch without climbing up?不上屋顶怎么测坡度？

From inside the attic, hold a level horizontally against a rafter, mark 12 in along the level, and measure straight up to the rafter. That vertical distance is the rise in a rise:12 pitch. A phone inclinometer app against the rafter also works.不用上屋顶：在阁楼里把水平尺贴着椽条水平放置，沿水平尺量出 12 英寸，再竖直量到椽条的垂直距离，这个数值就是 rise:12 中的 rise。也可以把手机倾角仪贴在椽条上读数。

Do I need to add for starter strip and ridge cap?起始条和脊瓦要另外算吗？

Yes, and they are sold separately. Budget the eave length for starter strip and the ridge plus hip length for cap shingles; the waste allowance in this tool covers cutting, not these separate products.需要，而且它们是单独售卖的。起始条按檐口长度估算，脊瓦按屋脊加斜脊长度估算；本工具中的损耗率只覆盖切割，不包含这些单独的产品。
