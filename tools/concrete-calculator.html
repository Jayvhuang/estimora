Concrete Calculator混凝土用量计算器

Concrete is the material people most often buy twice, because a slab that looks small on paper swallows an astonishing number of bags. This calculator turns your dimensions straight into volume and then into whole bags — or into the cubic yards you would order from a ready-mix truck.混凝土是最容易买错量的材料——图纸上不起眼的一块地坪，实际会吃掉惊人的袋数。这个计算器把你的尺寸直接换算成体积，再换算成整袋数量，或者换算成向搅拌车下单时使用的立方码／立方米。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Slab volume = length × width × thickness地坪体积 = 长 × 宽 × 厚

Round column volume = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × height圆柱体积 = π × (直径 ÷ 2)² × 高

Bags = volume × (1 + waste) ÷ bag yield, rounded up袋数 = 体积 × (1 + 损耗) ÷ 每袋产出量，向上取整

Bag yields follow the figures printed by pre-mixed concrete manufacturers: an 80 lb bag yields about 0.60 ft³, a 60 lb bag about 0.45 ft³ and a 40 lb bag about 0.30 ft³. Ready-mix suppliers normally sell in whole or half cubic yards and usually apply a short-load fee below about one yard, so for anything larger than roughly 1 yd³ (about 45 eighty-pound bags) it is worth pricing a delivery.袋产出量采用预拌混凝土厂家包装标注值：80 磅袋约 0.60 ft³，60 磅袋约 0.45 ft³，40 磅袋约 0.30 ft³。商品混凝土通常按整立方码或半立方码起订，低于约 1 立方码往往还要收小方量附加费；因此超过约 1 yd³（相当于 45 袋 80 磅装）时，值得先问一下送货报价。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

How many 80 lb bags of concrete are in a cubic yard?一立方码需要多少袋 80 磅混凝土？

A cubic yard is 27 ft³ and an 80 lb bag yields about 0.60 ft³, so it takes 45 bags — before any waste allowance. That is roughly 3,600 lb of material, which is why most people switch to ready-mix well before this point.一立方码等于 27 ft³，一袋 80 磅装约产出 0.60 ft³，因此需要 45 袋——这还没算损耗。总重约 3600 磅，所以大多数人在到达这个量之前就会改叫商品混凝土。

How thick should a concrete slab be?混凝土地坪应该做多厚？

Four inches (100 mm) is the usual thickness for a patio, shed base or residential walkway; driveways carrying vehicles are commonly poured at five to six inches (125–150 mm). Thickness affects volume linearly, so going from 4 in to 6 in adds 50% to your material cost. Structural slabs should be specified by an engineer.露台、工具房基础和住宅步道通常做 4 英寸（100 mm）厚；需要走车的车道一般做 5～6 英寸（125～150 mm）。体积与厚度成正比，从 4 英寸加到 6 英寸材料费直接增加 50%。结构性楼板应由工程师确定。

Should I add extra for spillage?需要额外留洒漏余量吗？

Yes. A 5–10% allowance covers uneven subgrade, spillage and slight over-excavation of footings. Footings dug by hand often need more, because the trench walls are rarely perfectly straight.需要。5～10% 的余量可以覆盖基底不平、洒漏以及基槽超挖。人工开挖的基槽往往要留更多，因为槽壁很难挖得笔直。
