Fence Calculator围栏材料计算器

A fence is a repeating pattern, which makes it easy to estimate and easy to get wrong by exactly one post. The rule is that a run of fence always has one more post than it has sections — and every one of those posts needs a hole full of concrete.围栏是重复结构，所以既容易估算，也容易正好算少一根柱子。规律是：一段围栏的立柱数永远比分段数多一根——而每一根柱子都需要一个灌满混凝土的柱坑。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Sections = fence length ÷ post spacing, rounded up分段数 = 围栏长度 ÷ 立柱间距，向上取整

Posts = sections + 1立柱数 = 分段数 + 1

Concrete per hole = π × (hole radius)² × depth − post volume每坑混凝土 = π × 坑半径² × 埋深 − 立柱占位体积

Post spacing of 6 to 8 ft is standard for timber fencing; wider spacing sags the rails. Bury roughly one third of the post length, or at least below the local frost line — in cold climates a shallow post will be lifted out of the ground over a few winters. The concrete figure subtracts the volume the post itself occupies in the hole, which is why it is lower than a plain cylinder calculation.木围栏的立柱间距常用 6～8 英尺，间距过大横档会下垂。埋深取柱长的约三分之一，并且至少要深于当地冻土线——寒冷地区埋得太浅的柱子几个冬天就会被冻胀顶出地面。混凝土用量已扣除立柱本身在坑内占据的体积，所以比按整个圆柱体计算的结果要小。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

How deep should fence posts go?围栏立柱应该埋多深？

One third of the above-ground height is the common rule of thumb, so a 6 ft fence uses posts buried about 2 ft, meaning 8 ft posts. In frost-prone regions the hole must reach below the frost line regardless of that ratio.常用经验是埋深取地上高度的三分之一，因此 6 英尺高的围栏埋深约 2 英尺，需要 8 英尺长的立柱。在有冻胀的地区，无论比例如何，坑深都必须超过当地冻土线。

How many bags of concrete per fence post?每根立柱要用多少袋混凝土？

A 10 in diameter hole 24 in deep holds about 1.1 ft³, and a 4×4 post occupies about 0.15 ft³ of that, so roughly 1 ft³ of concrete — around two 80 lb bags per hole.直径 10 英寸、深 24 英寸的柱坑容积约 1.1 ft³，4×4 立柱本身约占 0.15 ft³，因此实际约需 1 ft³ 混凝土——大约每坑两袋 80 磅装。

Do gates change the count?有门洞会影响数量吗？

Yes. A gate opening needs its own pair of posts, and the hinge post should be heavier and set deeper than a line post because it carries the gate's weight in cantilever.会。门洞需要单独一对立柱，其中合页侧的立柱应比普通立柱更粗、埋得更深，因为它要以悬臂方式承受门扇的重量。
