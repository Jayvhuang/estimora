Drywall Calculator石膏板用量计算器

Drywall is cheap per sheet and expensive per trip. This calculator gives the sheet count for the walls and, optionally, the ceiling, then adds the consumables that are always forgotten: screws, tape and joint compound.石膏板单张便宜，但来回跑一趟很贵。这个计算器会算出墙面（以及可选顶棚）需要的板数，并加上最容易被忘记的辅材：螺钉、纸带和接缝腻子。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Sheets = total area × (1 + waste) ÷ sheet area, rounded up张数 = 总面积 × (1 + 损耗) ÷ 单张面积，向上取整

Screws ≈ 32 per sheet at 16 in framing spacing螺钉 ≈ 每张 32 颗（龙骨间距 16 英寸）

Tape ≈ 400 ft per 1,000 ft² of board纸带 ≈ 每 1000 ft² 板面约 400 英尺

Longer sheets mean fewer butt joints, and butt joints are the hardest ones to hide, so use 12 ft board wherever it will physically fit through the door. Standard 1/2 in board suits most walls; use 5/8 in Type X where a fire-rated assembly is required and moisture-resistant board in bathrooms. Compound quantity depends heavily on finish level — a Level 5 skim coat can double it.板越长，对接缝越少，而对接缝是最难做平的，因此只要门能搬得进去就尽量用 12 英尺板。普通墙面用 1/2 英寸板即可；有防火要求的部位用 5/8 英寸 Type X，卫生间用防潮板。腻子用量与饰面等级关系很大——做满批（Level 5）用量可能翻倍。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

How many screws per drywall sheet?每张石膏板要打多少颗螺钉？

About 32 for a 4×8 sheet on framing at 16 in centres — roughly every 12 in in the field and every 8 in along the edges. Ceilings take more because gravity is working against you.龙骨间距 16 英寸时，4×8 板约需 32 颗——板中约每 12 英寸一颗，板边约每 8 英寸一颗。顶棚要更多，因为重力在跟你作对。

Should I hang drywall horizontally or vertically?石膏板应该横铺还是竖铺？

Horizontally on wood-framed walls in most of North America: it puts the tapered seam at a comfortable height and reduces total joint length. Vertical hanging is common on metal studs and in commercial fire-rated assemblies.在北美，木龙骨墙面多为横铺：坡口缝正好落在顺手的高度，接缝总长度也更短。轻钢龙骨和商业防火构造中竖铺更常见。

How much joint compound will I use?接缝腻子要用多少？

Around 0.053 lb per square foot of board for a standard three-coat taped finish. A 4.5 gal bucket covers roughly 450–500 ft². Skim coating the whole surface uses far more.标准三遍嵌缝饰面约每平方英尺板面 0.053 磅。一桶 4.5 加仑约可做 450～500 ft²。整面满批用量要大得多。
