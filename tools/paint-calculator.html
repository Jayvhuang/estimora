Paint Calculator油漆用量计算器

Most paint over-buying comes from ignoring two things: the openings you are not painting, and the fact that paint is sold in fixed can sizes. This calculator subtracts doors and windows, multiplies by your coat count, then rounds up to whole cans.买多油漆通常源于两点疏忽：忘了扣除不需要刷的门窗，以及忘了油漆是按固定桶装规格出售的。这个计算器会扣除门窗、乘以涂刷遍数，再按整桶向上取整。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Wall area = perimeter × ceiling height墙面面积 = 周长 × 层高

Paintable area = wall area − door area − window area可涂面积 = 墙面面积 − 门面积 − 窗面积

Volume = paintable area × coats ÷ spread rate涂料体积 = 可涂面积 × 遍数 ÷ 涂布率

A spread rate of 350 ft² per US gallon (about 8.6 m² per litre) is a common figure printed on interior latex paint, but it assumes a smooth, previously painted, non-porous surface. New drywall, bare plaster, heavy texture and deep colour changes all reduce coverage — sometimes by a third. Primer is a separate calculation: run the tool again with one coat and the primer's own spread rate.内墙乳胶漆标注的涂布率常见为 350 ft²/加仑（约 8.6 m²/升），但这是在光滑、已涂刷过、不吸水的基面上测得的。新石膏板、素水泥面、拉毛质感墙面以及深浅色大幅变化都会降低涂布率，有时可低三分之一。底漆需要单独计算：把遍数设为 1，并填入底漆自己的涂布率再算一次。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

How much paint do I need for a 12×12 room?12×12 的房间需要多少油漆？

With 8 ft ceilings that is a 48 ft perimeter × 8 ft = 384 ft² of wall. Subtract one door (about 21 ft²) and one window (about 15 ft²) and you have around 348 ft². Two coats at 350 ft²/gal is about 2.0 gallons, so you buy two gallons — and a third if the colour change is dramatic.层高 8 英尺时，周长 48 英尺 × 8 英尺 = 384 ft² 墙面。扣掉一扇门（约 21 ft²）和一扇窗（约 15 ft²）后约 348 ft²。按 350 ft²/加仑刷两遍约需 2.0 加仑，因此买 2 加仑即可；若颜色反差很大，再加一加仑。

Do I need to subtract doors and windows?一定要扣除门窗吗？

For one or two average openings the difference is small, but a room with sliding doors or a wall of glass can lose 15–25% of its paintable area. The calculator subtracts them so you are not buying paint for the glazing.只有一两个普通洞口时影响不大；但带推拉门或整面玻璃的房间，可涂面积可能减少 15～25%。计算器会自动扣除，避免你为玻璃买漆。

Does the ceiling count?顶棚算在里面吗？

No — this tool estimates walls only, because ceilings usually take a different product and often only one coat. Estimate a ceiling by running the tile or gravel-style area maths on the floor footprint and dividing by the spread rate.不计。本工具只估算墙面，因为顶棚通常用不同产品，而且经常只刷一遍。若要估算顶棚，可按地面投影面积除以涂布率单独计算。
