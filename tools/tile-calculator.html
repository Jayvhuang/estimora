Tile & Flooring Calculator瓷砖与地板用量计算器

The trap in tile maths is the grout joint. A 12×12 in tile with a 1/8 in joint actually occupies 12.125 × 12.125 in, and over a large floor that difference is worth several tiles. This calculator uses the effective size including the joint, then rounds up to whole boxes.瓷砖计算最容易忽略的是砖缝。12×12 英寸的砖配 1/8 英寸缝，实际占位是 12.125 × 12.125 英寸；面积一大，这个差值就是好几块砖。本计算器按含缝的有效尺寸计算，再按整箱向上取整。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Effective tile size = tile dimension + grout joint有效砖尺寸 = 砖尺寸 + 缝宽

Tiles = area × (1 + waste) ÷ effective tile area, rounded up块数 = 面积 × (1 + 损耗) ÷ 有效单块面积，向上取整

Boxes = tiles ÷ tiles per box, rounded up箱数 = 块数 ÷ 每箱块数，向上取整

Use 10% waste for a straight layout in a simple rectangular room, 15% where the room has alcoves or many cuts, and 20% or more for a diagonal or herringbone pattern, where almost every perimeter tile is cut twice. Buy the whole job from one production lot: shade varies between lots and a single replacement box bought later is often visibly different.简单矩形房间直铺按 10% 损耗；有壁龛、切割较多时按 15%；斜铺或人字铺按 20% 以上，因为周边几乎每块砖都要切两刀。整个工程尽量一次买齐同一批号：不同批次存在色差，事后单独补一箱往往肉眼可见。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

Does the grout line really change the tile count?砖缝真的会影响用砖量吗？

On a 200 ft² floor with 12 in tiles and a 1/8 in joint, the joint alone removes roughly four tiles' worth of coverage. It is not huge, but it is exactly the margin that sends people back to the store.以 200 ft² 地面、12 英寸砖、1/8 英寸缝为例，仅砖缝就相当于少铺约 4 块砖的面积。数量不大，但恰恰就是让人不得不再跑一趟建材店的那点差额。

How much extra tile should I keep?应该多留多少备用瓷砖？

Keep at least one full box after the job. Tiles get cracked by dropped tools years later, and matching a discontinued line is usually impossible.施工完成后至少留一整箱。日后掉落工具砸裂瓷砖是常事，而停产型号基本无法配到同款。

Can I use this for vinyl planks and laminate?这个工具能算 PVC 地板和强化地板吗？

Yes. Enter the plank length and width and set the joint to zero, since click-lock flooring has no grout line. Planks are sold by the box in square feet or square metres, so enter the coverage per box as the tiles-per-box figure if that is how your product is labelled.可以。填入地板的长和宽，并把缝宽设为 0，因为锁扣地板没有灰缝。地板通常按每箱平方英尺／平方米销售，如果产品是这样标注的，可把每箱覆盖面积换算成每箱块数填入。
