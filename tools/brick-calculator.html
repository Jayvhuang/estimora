Brick & Block Calculator砖与砌块用量计算器

Masonry counting works on nominal sizes, not actual ones. A modular brick is made at 7 5/8 × 2 1/4 in but is laid on a 3/8 in joint, so it occupies exactly 8 × 2 2/3 in in the wall — which is why the nominal figure is the one that divides cleanly into wall dimensions.砌体计算用的是名义尺寸，不是实际尺寸。模数砖出厂尺寸是 7 5/8 × 2 1/4 英寸，配 3/8 英寸灰缝砌筑后，在墙上正好占 8 × 2 2/3 英寸——这就是为什么名义尺寸能被墙体尺寸整除。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Nominal unit size = actual size + mortar joint名义尺寸 = 实际尺寸 + 灰缝

Units = wall area ÷ nominal face area × (1 + waste)块数 = 墙面面积 ÷ 名义面面积 × (1 + 损耗)

Mortar ≈ 1 bag per 30 standard bricks (3/8 in joint)砂浆 ≈ 每 30 块标准砖约 1 袋（3/8 英寸灰缝）

Roughly seven modular bricks cover one square foot of wall face in running bond, and a standard 8×8×16 in CMU covers about 0.89 ft². Allow 5% waste for a plain wall and more where there are openings, returns or a decorative bond pattern. Mortar consumption rises quickly with joint thickness — a 1/2 in joint uses noticeably more than a 3/8 in one, and hollow block laid with face-shell bedding uses far less than solid brick.错缝砌筑时每平方英尺墙面约需 7 块模数砖，标准 8×8×16 英寸混凝土砌块每块约占 0.89 ft²。平直墙面按 5% 损耗，有洞口、转角或装饰砌法时应增加。砂浆用量随灰缝厚度上升很快——1/2 英寸灰缝明显多于 3/8 英寸；空心砌块采用面壳铺浆时，用量远低于实心砖。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

How many bricks per square foot?每平方英尺墙面要多少块砖？

About 6.75 modular bricks per square foot of wall face in a single wythe with 3/8 in joints. Most people round to seven and add waste on top.单层墙、3/8 英寸灰缝时，每平方英尺墙面约 6.75 块模数砖。实际估算一般按 7 块计，再另加损耗。

How many blocks in a 8×16 ft wall?8×16 英尺的墙需要多少块砌块？

That is 128 ft², and a standard 8×8×16 in block covers about 0.89 ft² of face, so roughly 144 blocks before waste.墙面面积 128 ft²，标准 8×8×16 英寸砌块每块面积约 0.89 ft²，不含损耗约需 144 块。

Does this include the footing or reinforcement?包含基础和配筋吗？

No. Footings, rebar, grout-filled cores and lintels depend on the structural design and local code. This tool estimates the units and the bedding mortar only.不含。基础、钢筋、灌芯混凝土和过梁取决于结构设计与当地规范，本工具只估算砌块数量与砌筑砂浆。
