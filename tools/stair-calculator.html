Stair Calculator楼梯踏步计算器

Stairs are the one calculation in this set where being slightly wrong is a safety problem rather than a shopping problem. Risers must be equal — the code tolerance is 3/8 in across the whole flight — because people climb stairs by muscle memory, not by looking.这是本站里唯一一个「算错不是多跑一趟建材店，而是安全隐患」的计算。踢面必须等高——规范允许的整跑最大偏差是 3/8 英寸——因为人上下楼靠的是肌肉记忆，不是看着走。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Risers = round(total rise ÷ target riser)踢面数 = round(总高 ÷ 目标踢面高)

Actual riser = total rise ÷ risers; treads = risers − 1实际踢面 = 总高 ÷ 踢面数；踏面数 = 踢面数 − 1

Stringer length = √(total rise² + total run²)斜梁长度 = √(总高² + 总投影²)

The tool checks your result against limits widely used in residential construction — a maximum riser of 7 3/4 in and a minimum tread of 10 in, as set out in IRC 2021 §R311.7 — and also reports the Blondel comfort relation (2 × riser + tread, comfortable at about 24–25 in). These are planning aids. Codes differ by state, province and edition, and the authority having jurisdiction is the only one whose numbers count. Confirm before you cut a stringer.工具会按住宅建筑中广泛采用的限值检查结果——最大踢面 7 3/4 英寸、最小踏面 10 英寸（见 IRC 2021 §R311.7），同时给出 Blondel 舒适度关系式（2 × 踢面 + 踏面，约 24～25 英寸为舒适）。这些只是规划参考。各州／省及不同版本规范存在差异，最终以当地主管部门执行的规范为准。开料切斜梁前务必核实。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

Why must all risers be the same height?为什么每级踢面必须等高？

Because people climb by rhythm. A single riser that differs by more than about 3/8 in is a recognised trip hazard, and it is the most common stair defect flagged at inspection.因为人上下楼是按节奏走的。只要有一级踢面高度相差超过约 3/8 英寸，就构成公认的绊倒风险，这也是验收时最常见的楼梯缺陷。

Why is the last step 'missing'?为什么好像少了一级踏步？

There is always one fewer tread than risers, because the upper floor itself acts as the final tread. It looks like an error in the count until you draw it.踏面数永远比踢面数少一级，因为上层楼板本身就充当了最后一级踏面。不画图的时候看起来像算错了。

What is the Blondel rule?什么是 Blondel 公式？

A comfort relation from 18th-century French stair design: two risers plus one tread should total about 24 to 25 inches (630 mm). Stairs that satisfy the code but fall far outside this range tend to feel wrong underfoot.源自 18 世纪法国楼梯设计的舒适度经验公式：2 个踢面高加 1 个踏面宽应约等于 24～25 英寸（630 mm）。有些楼梯虽然符合规范，但偏离这个范围太远，走起来就是别扭。
