Deck Board Calculator木平台板材计算器

Deck boards are laid in rows, and each row occupies its own width plus the gap next to it. Get that gap wrong and you end up with a sliver board at the far edge — or a run to the lumber yard for two more boards.木平台的面板是一排排铺的，每一排占据「板宽 + 一条板缝」。板缝算错，最后一排要么剩下一条窄得难看的板，要么就得再跑一趟买两块。

The maths behind it背后的计算方法

Rows = deck depth ÷ (board width + gap), rounded up排数 = 平台进深 ÷ (板宽 + 缝宽)，向上取整

Boards = rows × (deck length ÷ board length, rounded up)板数 = 排数 × (平台长度 ÷ 单板长度，向上取整)

Joists = deck length ÷ joist spacing + 1龙骨数 = 平台长度 ÷ 龙骨间距 + 1

A nominal 5/4×6 deck board is actually about 5.5 in wide, and a 2×6 is the same — always estimate on the actual width, which is what this tool uses. Gaps of 1/8 to 1/4 in allow drainage and let the boards move; wet pressure-treated lumber shrinks as it dries, so it can be laid tighter than kiln-dried or composite boards. Joist spacing of 16 in on centre suits most decking, but many composite products require 12 in when installed diagonally.名义 5/4×6 的地板实际宽约 5.5 英寸，2×6 也是同样宽度——估算必须用实际宽度，本工具即按实际宽度计算。板缝取 1/8～1/4 英寸以便排水和让木材伸缩；含水率高的防腐木干燥后会收缩，因此可以铺得比窑干木或塑木更紧。龙骨间距 16 英寸适用于大多数地板，但不少塑木产品在斜铺时要求 12 英寸。

Questions about this calculator关于这个计算器的常见问题

What gap should I leave between deck boards?木平台板之间应该留多大缝？

About 1/8 in for wet pressure-treated lumber, which shrinks as it dries, and 1/4 in for dry or composite boards, which do not. Zero gap is a mistake in any material — water needs somewhere to go.含水率高的防腐木留约 1/8 英寸，因为干燥后会收缩；已干燥的木材或塑木留 1/4 英寸。任何材料都不应该零缝——水总得有地方排走。

How many screws per deck board?每块面板要打多少颗螺钉？

Two per board at every joist crossing. A 16 ft board over joists at 16 in centres crosses 13 joists, so 26 screws — which is how a small deck quietly consumes a thousand fasteners.每块板在每根龙骨交叉处打 2 颗。16 英尺长的板、龙骨间距 16 英寸，会跨过 13 根龙骨，即 26 颗——一个小平台就这样悄无声息地吃掉上千颗螺钉。

Does this include framing, posts and footings?包含龙骨架、立柱和基础吗？

It estimates deck boards, joists and screws. Beams, posts, footings and hardware depend on span tables and local code, so size them with a span table or have the frame designed.只估算面板、龙骨和螺钉。梁、立柱、基础和五金件取决于跨度表和当地规范，请查跨度表或请人做结构设计。
